Precision Engineered Screens

Designed for efficient airflow and dependable fluid bed drying applications.

01 / OVERVIEW

Dinco Industries Fluid Dryer Screens are designed for fluid bed drying applications where controlled airflow and effective material processing are essential. Engineered for demanding industrial environments, these screens help support consistent air distribution and reliable drying operations.

02 / APPLICATIONS

Fluid Dryer Screens are used in fluid bed processing systems across pharmaceutical, chemical, food processing and other industrial sectors. Depending on the equipment design and material requirements, they can support drying, granulation and powder processing applications.

03 / PERFORMANCE & EFFICIENCY

Properly designed fluid dryer screens help maintain suitable airflow through the product bed and support uniform material processing. Screen opening, material selection and construction can be tailored to the application to help achieve dependable operation and effective drying performance.

04 / QUALITY & SUPPORT

Dinco Industries provides industrial screening solutions with a focus on application requirements, suitable material selection and dependable product construction. Screen specifications can be discussed according to equipment compatibility, operating conditions and maintenance requirements.
